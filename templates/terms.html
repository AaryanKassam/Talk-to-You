Terms

TTYS is a personal project that runs locally. It is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind.

What it does

It builds a statistical n-gram model from a chat export and suggests likely next words. It does not understand meaning, and it will happily reproduce whatever was in the source text, including things you would not want to send to anyone. Read what it suggests before you accept it.

Your responsibility

Only upload exports you are entitled to have. Group chat exports contain other people's writing, and the fact that a message was sent to you does not make it yours to publish. You are responsible for how you use anything this tool generates.
